  RELEASE NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Developed by SEGA's Amusement Vision and published by Nintendo, F-Zero GX
  brings blistering 60fps anti-gravity racing to the Nintendo GameCube.
  Featuring 30 pilots, over 20 tracks across four cups, a full Story Mode,
  custom machine garage parts, and F-Zero AX arcade memory card connectivity.

  This release is preserved in NKit.iso format, retaining full disc header
  integrity, banner data, and streaming audio tracks while stripping unused
  garbage padding for clean archival and hardware/emulator compatibility.
