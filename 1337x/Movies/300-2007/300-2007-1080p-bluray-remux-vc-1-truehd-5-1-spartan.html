Why VC-1 Remux?

When *300* debuted during the HD-DVD vs. Blu-ray format battle in 2007, Warner Bros. encoded the master in VC-1 to accommodate dual-format releases. Many remux collectors prefer this authentic untouched encode to retain Snyder’s iconic gritty comic-book contrast without modern re-compression artifacts.

Dolby TrueHD 5.1 Audio

Features the original lossless 5.1 TrueHD master audio track (with full backwards-compatible AC3 640 kbps core). Heavy low-frequency effects (LFE) tuned for the legendary battle cries, clashing bronze shields, and Tyler Bates’ orchestral score.
