Synopsis & Plot

Spirit: Stallion of the Cimarron is a 2002 American animated adventure drama film directed by Kelly Asbury and Lorna Cook in their directorial debuts, and written by John Fusco. The film follows the adventures of a wild Kiger Mustang stallion named Spirit, who is captured by the U.S. Cavalry during the American Old West frontier expansion. He refuses to be broken in spirit, even as he befriends a young Lakota man who helps him escape and reunite with his herd and mare, Rain.
